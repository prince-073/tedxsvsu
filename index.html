Meet Our Speakers

VK
V.C. Dinesh Kumar
Vice Chancellor, Shri Vishwakarma Skill University (SVSU)

Leading India's first Government Skill University — ranked 3rd nationally in 2025 — Prof. Dinesh Kumar is transforming how India thinks about education. His vision bridges skill-based learning with Industry 4.0, empowering youth in Robotics, AI, EVs, and Semiconductors to become job-ready innovators and entrepreneurs.

RN
Dr. Raj Nehru
OSD to CM of Haryana | Director General, SJHIFM

A visionary at the intersection of policy, education, and skill development, Dr. Raj Nehru founded India's first Government Skill University and shaped the NEP 2020 framework. Today, as OSD to Haryana's Chief Minister and DG of SJHIFM, he is driving the state's future through data-led fiscal policy and youth empowerment.

KS
Kavitha S
Software Enterprise Architect

A distinguished Software Enterprise Architect and model by passion from Bengaluru, Kavitha represents a powerful blend of intellect, resilience, and elegance. Crowned Mrs. India 2025, she advocates emotional wellness, self-growth, and women's empowerment.

MA
Manish Anand
IT Expert

A technologist and community builder, Manish believes innovation becomes meaningful only when it improves lives. His work spans AI-driven solutions, global engineering dialogue, IEEE leadership, mentoring, and community-first impact.

DV
Dr. Deepak M. Vikhe, PhD
Prosthodontist, Implantologist & Innovator

An Associate Professor at Rural Dental College, PIMS, Dr. Vikhe is an award-winning dental innovator, researcher, author, and educator whose work focuses on practical, affordable healthcare solutions born from empathy and real human needs.

CS
Coach Sandeep
Growth & Mindset Coach | Social Media Strategist

A Growth & Mindset Coach, TEDx Speaker, and Social Media Strategist, Coach Sandeep has trained over 1 lakh people to think bigger, monetize their skills, and leverage digital opportunities to build a better future.

MA
Manan Aggarwal
Certified Speedcuber

A 9-year-old certified speedcuber and State-level Abacus Champion from Delhi, Manan explores how a scrambled cube represents possibility. His talk challenges the urge to quickly fix messiness, showing how confusion sparks discovery.

BS
Binita Shrivastava
Founder, Visionara Global

Empowering 100,000+ women through leadership, entrepreneurship, and transformational pageantry while building one of India's fastest-growing women empowerment ecosystems.

RI
Ritu Insan
Founder, Talented Ritu Insan

With 20+ years of experience and 50+ branches across India, Ritu has trained 4 lakh+ women in fashion, stitching, and garment making. Her mission of Mahila Sashaktikaran drives her to turn every woman's skill into confidence, income, and independence.

SM
Sourav Mahajan
Founder, Talented Sourav Mahajan

With 20+ years of experience and 50+ branches across India, Sourav has trained 4 lakh+ women in fashion, stitching, and garment making. His mission of Mahila Sashaktikaran drives him to turn every woman's skill into confidence, income, and independence.

SS
Shivani Sharma
Founder, Winq Cosmetics

At 26, Shivangi built Winq Cosmetics to bridge the gap between aspiration and affordability in Indian beauty. Her mission: bring quality, inclusive cosmetics to women in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities who deserve premium beauty without the premium price tag.

NP
Neera Paritosh
Life Coach, Author & Educator

With 34 years as an educator and a powerful post-pandemic reinvention, Neera channels life coaching, spirituality, and parenting wisdom to help people transform from within. Author of Ripple of Words and Parenting the Parent, she believes real change begins with a shift in mindset.

KM
Khushali Mehta
Entrepreneur & Social Activist

Khushali is a passionate entrepreneur and social activist dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities. Her work focuses on creating sustainable solutions for education, healthcare, and economic development in rural areas.

ST
Suryasnata Tripathy
Assistant Professor, IIT Ropar | Author & Filmmaker

A scientist by day and storyteller by soul, Suryasnata bridges Biomedical Engineering research at IIT Ropar with Odia literature, theatre, and cinema. Sahitya Akademi Yuva Puraskar winner, playwright, and film producer — he explores what it means to be both rigorous and deeply human.
